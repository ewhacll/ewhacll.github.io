In Part 2, she further emphasized that the most important foundation for children’s language development is rich, high-quality communication in the language parents can use most naturally. The series highlighted how parents can nurture children’s language sense, thinking skills, and communication abilities through meaningful everyday interaction rather than forced early English exposure."
